Baligen Talihati

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, advised by Professor Hao Huang. My research lies at the intersection of AI4Science and intelligent infrastructure systems, using machine learning to model complex physical and cyber-physical systems under uncertainty. My earlier work focused on community energy systems and the adoption of shared energy storage; I currently work on integrating AI with distributed energy resources (DER) to improve grid flexibility and resilience. Looking ahead, I aim to extend these data-driven scheduling and optimization methods to data center operations, developing AI-based frameworks that jointly optimize energy use, workload scheduling, and system resilience across both power grids and computing infrastructure. My work sits at the intersection of deep learning, optimization, and network data science, addressing key challenges in the evolution of intelligent, AI-driven physical systems.

Improving energy storage sharing efficiency through reliable communication

A communication-reliable hierarchical energy storage sharing framework was developed, increasing the scheduling success rate from 61.8% to 97.8% under imperfect communication conditions. Paper

Proposing a dynamic carbon network model for IES

Proposed the concept of energy–carbon flow mapping by integrating carbon emission factors and utilizing network analysis, demonstrated through a case study in an industrial park in Nanjing. Paper

Managing community energy systems using multi-agent reinforcement learning

Utilized MARL to manage community-shared energy storage charging/discharging, intelligent EV charging, and dynamic pricing, with the goal of alleviating pressure on the community distribution network. Paper

Establishing a consistency evaluation framework for different paradigms of shared energy storage

A consistent evaluation framework is proposed for diversified battery energy storage use scenarios. The operational cost of a community with various controllable loads is optimized to find the optimal storage solution. Paper
